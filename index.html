04 — COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions,
answered.

Do you work with both employers and job seekers?+

Yes. The service concept includes hiring support for firms, professional profiles and career introductions for job seekers, and industry partnerships.

Can firms request contract or project-based support?+

Yes. Firms can describe direct-hire, contract, temporary, or project-based needs. Availability and engagement terms are discussed individually.

How do skills assessments work?+

Assessments may include software proficiency, portfolio or work-sample reviews, and task-based exercises. Specific assessments and training programs will be developed with employers.

Are there active jobs and guaranteed placements?+

This is an agency-services preview. Live job listings, placements, program enrollment, and employer agreements are not yet connected on this page. No outcome is guaranteed.
